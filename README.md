# Unavailable in India: political social-media takedowns, 2026

**OSINT JOURNO · v1 · 12 November 2026** · companion dataset to *Case File 05: Unavailable in India*

136 documented takedowns, withholdings and account restrictions of political content in India, January to October 2026, with a focus on the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) protests of 20 July and 10 October. Each case links to the published report it comes from.

## Method
Cases come from published reports (Alt News, Newslaundry, the Internet Freedom Foundation, The Wire, ThePrint, Outlook, Free Press Journal, CPJ, SFLC.in and others). Every row was re-checked against its source; 70 rows had at least one field corrected and the change is recorded in `verification_note`. Evidence levels separate cases where the platform notice was reported from cases that rest on the account holder's own account.

## Limits
This is **not a complete list**. Section 69A blocking orders are confidential, Section 79(3)(b) notices go through the Sahyog portal without the user, and most takedowns are never reported. A row records what was reported, not a finding that a takedown was lawful or unlawful. Police and government say such actions target rumours, deepfakes and abuse.

To look for takedown requests yourself, search the [Lumen Database](https://lumendatabase.org), a project of Harvard's Berkman Klein Center.
